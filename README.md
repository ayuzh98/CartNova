## 🛒 About CartNova

**CartNova** is a modern e-commerce website developed as a college project.

The goal of CartNova is to provide a clean, responsive and user-friendly shopping experience where users can explore products, view product details, manage their cart and proceed through checkout.

> **Shop Smart. Live Better.**
